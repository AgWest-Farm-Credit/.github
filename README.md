# AgWest Farm Credit

**Grounded by tradition. Inspired by possibility.**

AgWest Farm Credit is an agricultural lending cooperative serving farmers, ranchers, timber harvesters, aquatic producers, and rural communities throughout the West.

We provide consistent, reliable credit, insurance, and financial services to the people who feed and clothe the world.

## Our purpose

At AgWest Farm Credit, we champion agriculture by serving as the most trusted resource, helping our customers, employees, and their communities thrive.
